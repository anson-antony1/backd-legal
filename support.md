# Backd — Support

## Common questions

**What is Backd?**
A private accountability app. Set a goal, check in by your deadline, optionally
back it with proof (a photo, screenshot, or note), and a friend you choose can
review it. No public feed, no leaderboards.

**Is my data private?**
Yes — private by default. Only you see your data, except the specific proof you
ask a friend to review. We don't sell data or use it for ads. See our Privacy
Policy: https://anson-antony1.github.io/backd-legal/privacy.

**Does Backd cost money / charge my card?**
No. All features are free, and Backd does not process payments. "Stakes" are a
number you track yourself for motivation.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and
all data and cannot be undone.

**Why does Backd ask for Health / Location / Camera?**
Only for the proof types you choose, and only in context: Camera/Photos for photo
proof; Apple Health (read-only) to verify a step/workout goal; Location for a
single check-in capture to verify a place. You can decline any of these and still
use the app, and revoke them anytime in iOS Settings.

**Can I connect Google Calendar or Canvas?**
Yes, both are optional. In Settings → Integrations you can connect Google Calendar
(to turn upcoming events into goals, or add goals to your calendar) and Canvas (to
import upcoming assignments as goals). Connecting either is your choice; importing
only pre-fills the goal screen — Backd never creates a goal without you confirming.
You can disconnect either at any time, which deletes the stored connection.

**What is the "Run AI check" button on proof?**
An optional, advisory second opinion. When you tap it, the proof is checked by an
AI and you get a suggestion — but you or your assigned friend always make the final
call. It never runs on its own, and you don't have to use it.

**Can I make text bigger or increase contrast?**
Yes. Backd follows your iPhone's Larger Text (Display & Text Size) setting, and
Settings → Accessibility adds High contrast and Flat surfaces (reduce transparency)
options.

**A reminder showed on my lock screen — does it reveal my goals?**
No. Notification text is deliberately generic and never includes your specific
goal or any money figures.
